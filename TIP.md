# Tip — pay what the review was worth

Some reviews here are free. We pick a public file, review it, and post the findings on a
public thread — unasked, unpaid, and with the same standard as the paid ones.

If one of those was worth something to you, this is where you say so.

**[Tip →](https://buy.stripe.com/3cI14m70Tck9f6ga8hbEA03)** — you choose the amount, CA$3 minimum.

## What a tip buys

Nothing, and that is the point of it.

A tip is for a review you have already read. It does not put you in a queue, does not buy
a review of your own file, and does not create an obligation in either direction. If you
do want your own file looked at, that is the [CA$2 slot](README.md), and it comes with a
turnaround promise and a refund right. This page has neither, because there is nothing
here left to deliver.

## Why CA$3 is the floor

Stripe takes 2.9% + CA$0.30 of whatever you send. Below CA$3 the fee is a large fraction
of the tip, and we would rather you keep the money than split it with a processor. CA$3 is
the minimum, not the suggestion — the amount is yours.

## Tax

A voluntary gratuity is not payment for a supply, so no GST/HST is charged and no
registration number appears on your receipt. What you enter is what is charged.

## If you tipped by mistake

Say so — [open an issue](../../issues/new) or email **support@zitacron.com** — and we send
it back. We will not ask you why.

## Who receives it

Zitacron Inc., Ontario, Canada. Stripe passes us your name and email with the payment; we
use them for the accounting record Canadian tax rules require, and for nothing else. Card
details go to Stripe and never reach us.

The tip checkout asks you to accept **[TERMS.md](TERMS.md)**. The part that applies to you
is **clause 11** — a tip buys nothing, and we send it back if you ask. Clauses 1 to 5 are
the paid review's turnaround and refund rights, and a tip is not a purchase, so they do
not apply here.
